Map Controls

Map counts are derived from the dashboard initiative data. Because 15 initiatives have 'N/A' geographic coverage, the map includes two viewing modes: 'Explicit regional coverage only' and 'Treat N/A as nationwide'.
How to interpret this map
Explicit regional coverage only counts initiatives only where a region is specifically listed.
Treat N/A as nationwide adds initiatives with unspecified coverage to every region.
